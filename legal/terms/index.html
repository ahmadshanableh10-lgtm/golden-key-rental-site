These terms govern the rental of any vehicle from Golden Key Rent Car LLC. By submitting a booking request you accept them.

Eligibility

The driver must be at least 21 years old, or 25 for vehicles in our Luxury, Sports and VIP categories.

UAE residents must hold a valid UAE driving licence and Emirates ID. Visitors must present a passport, entry stamp or visa, their home-country licence and, where their nationality requires it, an International Driving Permit.

Rental period and rates

A rental day is 24 hours from the time of collection or delivery. Returning more than 59 minutes late may incur an additional day at the daily rate.

Where a rental spans a full week or month, we apply the corresponding period rate automatically. We will never charge more than the flat daily rate multiplied by the number of days.

Mileage

Each vehicle carries its own daily kilometre allowance, shown on that vehicle’s page. Allowances are pooled across the rental — a 250 km/day car rented for four days gives you 1,000 km total.

Kilometres above the allowance are charged at the per-kilometre rate published on the vehicle page. Vehicles marked "Unlimited" carry no distance restriction.

Security deposit

A refundable deposit is held against the driver’s credit card at collection. The amount is published on each vehicle page.

The deposit is released after return, once tolls, fines and any damage assessment have been settled. Release timing depends on your card issuer and is typically 7–21 days.

Tolls, fines and fuel

Salik and Darb tolls are charged at cost. Traffic fines are billed at the issued amount plus an administration charge.

Vehicles are supplied with a full tank and must be returned full, or refuelling is charged at cost plus a service fee.

Insurance and liability

Comprehensive insurance is included. An excess applies to any claim; optional zero-excess cover may be purchased at collection.

Insurance is void where the driver was unlicensed, under the influence, racing, off-roading in a vehicle not designated for it, or where the vehicle was driven by a person not named on the agreement.

Cross-border travel

Taking a vehicle outside the UAE requires written permission and additional cover arranged in advance. It is not available on all vehicles.
