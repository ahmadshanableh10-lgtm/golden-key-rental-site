What documents do I need to rent a car in Dubai?

UAE residents need an Emirates ID and a valid UAE driving licence. Visitors need their passport, visit visa, home-country licence and — depending on nationality — an International Driving Permit. We confirm exactly what applies to you before delivery.

Is there a mileage limit?

Every car shows its own daily kilometre allowance on its page — it is not the same flat number across the fleet. Economy cars typically carry 250–300 km per day, supercars 100–200 km, and a few long-lease cars are unlimited. Any extra-kilometre fee is shown on the same page, in dirhams per km.

Do you deliver the car to me?

Yes. Delivery within Dubai is free. We also deliver to Abu Dhabi and Sharjah, to both airports, and to your hotel or residence. Choose your pick-up point when you reserve.

What is the security deposit?

The deposit is held on your credit card and released after the car is returned and any Salik or fines are settled. The exact amount is listed on each car page — it ranges from AED 1,000 on economy cars to AED 20,000 on our top luxury models.

Is insurance included?

Comprehensive insurance is included with every rental. An excess applies in the event of a claim, and optional zero-excess cover can be added at pick-up.

Can I pay online?

Reserving online places a booking request and holds the car. Payment is taken at delivery or pick-up, so you can confirm the vehicle and the paperwork in person first.
