About Golden Key

Rent the car. Keep the day simple.

Golden Key Rent Car LLC is a Dubai-based rental company operating across Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah. We rent everything from a AED 100-a-day Attrage to a Rolls-Royce Ghost — and we treat both customers the same way.

Most rental companies in the UAE hide the real cost until you're at the desk: a teaser daily rate, a mileage cap nobody mentions, an excess that appears on the paperwork. We publish the daily, weekly and monthly rate for every car, the exact kilometre allowance for that specific vehicle, the extra-kilometre charge, and the deposit — before you book.

Delivery inside Dubai is free. Book online and we'll confirm within thirty minutes; no account to create, and nothing charged until you have the keys.
